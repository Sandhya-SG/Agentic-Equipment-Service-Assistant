## General Safety Principles
All service on the XR-500 must be performed in Maintenance Mode with the servo drives de-energized. Never bypass a safety interlock. Never place hands inside the gantry envelope while any drive is energized. If a task requires access to energized circuits, it must be escalated to a qualified engineer and is outside the scope of routine field service.

## Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Hazards
Semiconductor devices and the contactor socket are ESD-sensitive. Before handling devices or touching the contactor pins, connect an ESD wrist strap to the handler ground point and work on an ESD-safe surface. Failure to observe ESD precautions can damage devices and the socket, and is treated as a mandatory precaution, not an optional one.

## High Voltage and Stored Energy
The power distribution unit and the thermal conditioning heater operate at high voltage. Service of these subsystems requires lockout/tagout and is restricted to qualified engineers. Field service engineers must not open the power distribution enclosure. The thermal unit may retain stored energy in its capacitors after power off; a mandatory discharge wait of five minutes applies before any access, and this work must be escalated rather than performed in the field.

## Thermal and Pressurized Hazards
The thermal conditioning unit surfaces can reach high temperatures. Allow surfaces to cool and confirm with the console temperature readout before contact. The vacuum and pneumatic lines are pressurized during operation; isolate and vent pressure before servicing pneumatic components.

## Required PPE Summary
Routine handler service requires safety glasses and an ESD wrist strap at minimum. Thermal unit work additionally requires heat-resistant gloves once escalation and lockout are complete. Any task touching high voltage, stored energy, or the power distribution enclosure is not field-serviceable and must be escalated to a qualified engineer.
