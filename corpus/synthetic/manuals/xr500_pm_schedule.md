## Overview
This document defines the scheduled preventive maintenance (PM) tasks for the
XR-500 handler. Following the PM schedule reduces unplanned downtime and keeps the
handler within specification. All PM tasks are performed in Maintenance Mode.

## Daily Checks
At the start of each shift, confirm the handler enters Maintenance Mode cleanly
and the operator console reports no active faults. Visually inspect the pick-head
nozzle for debris and confirm the vacuum gauge reads within the working range
during a grip self-test.

## 500-Hour Maintenance
Every 500 operating hours, replace the pick-head vacuum filter cartridge per SOP.
Inspect the gantry drive belt for wear and confirm tension is within range.
Inspect the contactor pin field under magnification for flattening or debris.

## 2000-Hour Maintenance
Every 2000 operating hours, perform the coolant check on the thermal conditioning
unit and top up or replace coolant as needed. Perform a full motion calibration in
Diagnostic Mode. Inspect all accessible connectors for security and corrosion.

## Escalation Items
Certain PM-adjacent tasks are not field-serviceable and must be scheduled with a
qualified engineer: any service of the high-voltage power distribution unit, the
thermal heater element, or tasks requiring access to stored-energy components.
These require lockout/tagout and are outside routine field PM.
