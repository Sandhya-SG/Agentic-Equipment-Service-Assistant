## Purpose and Scope
This SOP describes the procedure to replace the pick-head vacuum filter cartridge on the XR-500 handler. It applies when the vacuum gauge reads below 60 kPa during a grip self-test, or as part of scheduled preventive maintenance every 500 operating hours.

## Prerequisites
Confirm the handler is in Maintenance Mode. Confirm the gantry servo drives are de-energized (automatic on entering Maintenance Mode). Have a replacement filter cartridge (part XR5-VF-02) and a lint-free cloth available.

## Procedure
Enter Maintenance Mode from the operator console. Open the pick-head service panel. Locate the vacuum filter housing on the left side of the pick head. Rotate the housing cap counter-clockwise and withdraw the spent cartridge. Insert the new cartridge with the flow arrow pointing toward the nozzle. Reseat the housing cap and rotate clockwise until it seats. Run a grip self-test in Diagnostic Mode and confirm the vacuum gauge reads within the 80-90 kPa working range.

## Verification
A successful replacement shows a stable vacuum reading in the working range across three consecutive grip self-tests. If the reading remains low, inspect the nozzle tip for cracks and check for vacuum line leaks before returning the handler to Production Mode.
