## Purpose and Scope
This SOP covers homing and calibrating the motorized stage on the VP-200. Use it
when the station reports positioning errors, misframed images, or after any
mechanical service to the stage.

## Prerequisites
Enter Calibration Mode. The stage moves during this procedure, so confirm the
optics enclosure is closed and no obstructions are on the stage rails. Have a
lint-free cloth available for rail cleaning.

## Procedure
From Calibration Mode, select the stage homing routine. Observe the stage move to
its home position and confirm the home sensor indicator turns green. If homing
fails, open Service Mode, inspect the home sensor for debris or obstruction, and
wipe the rails with a lint-free cloth. Return to Calibration Mode and re-run
homing. Once homing succeeds, run the stage alignment routine and confirm the
reported position error is within tolerance.

## Verification
A successful calibration shows the home sensor triggering reliably across three
homing cycles and a position error within the stated tolerance. If homing remains
intermittent, the home sensor may be failing and should be replaced per the
service manual.
