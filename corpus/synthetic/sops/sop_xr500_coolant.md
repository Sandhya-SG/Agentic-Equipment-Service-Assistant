## Purpose and Scope
This SOP covers checking and restoring coolant circulation on the XR-500 thermal
conditioning unit. Use it when the station reports Fault E-402 (Coolant Flow Low)
or when devices arrive at the socket outside the target temperature window.

## Prerequisites
Enter Maintenance Mode. Note that the thermal unit surfaces may be hot; confirm
the console temperature readout shows a safe surface temperature before touching
any component. Have coolant of the approved type available. Do not service the
heater element — that involves high voltage and stored energy and must be
escalated to a qualified engineer.

## Procedure
Enter Maintenance Mode and allow the thermal unit to cool. Confirm the coolant
reservoir level is within the marked range; top up with approved coolant if low.
Inspect the coolant lines for kinks or leaks. Confirm the circulation pump runs
during a thermal self-test in Diagnostic Mode. If the pump runs but flow remains
low, the coolant filter may be clogged and should be replaced.

## Verification
A successful check shows the coolant reservoir in range, no line leaks, and the
thermal unit reaching setpoint within the expected time during a self-test. If the
unit still cannot reach setpoint and coolant flow is confirmed, the heater element
may have failed — escalate to a qualified engineer, as heater service is not a
field task.
