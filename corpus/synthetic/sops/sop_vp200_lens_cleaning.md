## Purpose and Scope
This SOP describes cleaning the camera-head lens on the VP-200 vision inspection
station. Use it when images appear blurred, hazy, or show fixed spots across all
inspected devices, or as part of scheduled maintenance every 250 operating hours.

## Prerequisites
Enter Service Mode and confirm the camera head is parked and the stage servo is
disabled. Have the approved optical wipes (part VP2-OW-01) and optical-grade
solvent available. Wear powder-free gloves to avoid transferring oils to the lens.

## Procedure
Enter Service Mode from the operator console. Open the optics enclosure. Using a
blower, remove loose dust from the lens surface first — never wipe dry dust, as it
can scratch the coating. Apply a small amount of optical solvent to a fresh wipe
and clean the lens in a single spiral motion from center to edge. Use a fresh wipe
for each pass. Allow the lens to dry fully before closing the enclosure.

## Verification
Return to Calibration Mode and run the focus-and-clarity check against the
calibration target. A clean lens produces a sharp target image with no fixed
spots. If haze or spots persist after two cleaning attempts, the lens coating may
be damaged and the camera head should be inspected for replacement.
